# Flow 1 Material Variant — Cyan Blank

Use this variant only after the user selects **青色素体**.

## Input roles

- `Image 1 = accepted person cutout; sole reference for identity, pose, action, silhouette, clothing masses, and character-specific content.`
- `Image 2 = assets/flow-1-cyan-material-board.png; inspect only the glossy turquoise-cyan vinyl regions for color, highlights, and optical-depth cues.`

Image 2 is a material board, not a character reference. Ignore its black hair and shoes, red background shapes, yellow/red eyes, white teeth, dark painted cavities, monster/cat/bat/skull anatomy, claws, costume details, pose, proportions, silhouette, and composition. Never transfer any of those elements.

## Color and finish

Make the complete toy an unpainted monochrome **bright turquoise-cyan sofubi blank**: a saturated cool green-blue close to the cyan vinyl regions in Image 2. Face, hair, skin, clothing, accessories, hands, legs, and shoes must all use this same cyan vinyl.

No black hair or shoes. No red, yellow, white, or black painted details. No colored eyes, teeth, skull, wings, monster features, decals, gradients, or secondary colors. Shallow facial impressions may become darker only through natural form shadow in the same cyan material; they must not look black-painted.

Match only the cyan surface's smooth, highly glossy, oil-polished soft-vinyl response: crisp near-white highlight cores, broad curved reflections, rounded hotspots, saturated cyan midtones, and slightly deeper teal-cyan turning shadows. Keep the material plump, optically deep, clean, and dry.

Reject blue, navy, emerald green, mint pastel, pale aqua, gray cyan, matte turquoise, satin plastic, powder, chalk, wax, dry rubber, frosted material, flat diffuse-only shading, foggy bloom, weak highlights, or overexposure.

## Material-only correction

If a correct sculpt looks dull or drifts away from cyan, edit only its finish and color response. Preserve geometry, pose, proportions, face, silhouette, framing, and background. Restore one uniform turquoise-cyan vinyl across every part without importing any non-cyan element from the references.
