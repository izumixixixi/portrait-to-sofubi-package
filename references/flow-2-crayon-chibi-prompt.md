## Character Design

The character must closely follow the figure design language of Image 2:

- extremely short chibi proportions, about **2.5–3 heads tall**
- very large head and very small body
- flat, highly simplified face
- **two huge solid dark blue-violet oval eyes**
- eyes placed low and fairly wide apart
- **no eyelashes, no highlights, no iris detail, no polished anime eyes**
- **a tiny horizontal oval / bean-shaped mouth**
- almost no nose
- hair simplified into a few large chunky shapes
- slightly awkward, naive, asymmetrical cuteness

## Linework

The linework must look like **rough crayon / oil pastel drawing**, not clean digital line art:

- thick and grainy outlines
- slightly wobbly and uneven
- repeated strokes
- occasional gaps or misalignment
- colored outlines in deep blue-violet / indigo rather than clean black ink

## Coloring

The coloring must feel hand-drawn:

- uneven fill
- visible paper texture
- patchy crayon coverage
- some white paper showing through
- slight coloring outside the lines
- rough waxy texture

Use a **limited pastel palette** with only a few essential colors.

Do not make the image too colorful.

## Background

The background should be **extremely simplified**, with only a few loose casual marks suggesting the environment.

It should look like a few quick doodles rather than a fully rendered scene.

## Style Priority

Important: study **Image 2’s drawing language**, especially:

- facial construction
- eye design
- mouth design
- character proportions
- line quality
- coloring texture
- simplified background treatment

Do **not** copy the exact character, pose, objects, or scene from Image 2.

## Avoid

Avoid:

- polished chibi art
- standard anime eyes
- smooth digital outlines
- clean flat color
- too many colors
- detailed backgrounds
- 3D rendering
- realistic anatomy
- text
- logos
- signatures
- watermarks
