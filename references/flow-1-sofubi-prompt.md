## Simple, Vintage, Cute Face — Hard Requirement

The face is a primitive vintage toy symbol, not a miniature portrait. It must be **simpler, more retro, and cuter than every realistic facial cue in the source**. If likeness conflicts with this face style, sacrifice facial likeness.

- one short, shallow incised line or tiny soft arc for the mouth; no lips, teeth, gums, or open mouth

At thumbnail size, the face should read like a friendly old hand-sculpted mascot: naive, slightly awkward, calm, and charming. It must not read like a person whose realistic face was coated in colored vinyl.
